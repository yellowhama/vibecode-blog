# 풀 타임 유튜브 영상용 AI 비디오 프롬프트 세트 (Phase 1 Act 1)

**목표:** 블로그 전문 낭독본(`phase1_act1_script_and_scenes.md`)에 정의된 총 10개의 Act(씬)에 맞춰, Nano Banana 및 Google Video(Veo 등)에서 사용할 **실전 모션 프롬프트**를 작성합니다.

---

## 🌟 공통 스타일 및 에셋 룰 (복사/붙여넣기 전용)

영상 생성 시 일관성을 유지하기 위해 모든 프롬프트는 아래의 기본 설정을 상속해야 합니다.

*   **[Style & Motion Base]:** `Stop-motion claymation, Aardman studio style. Soft matte studio lighting. Heavy fingerprint textures, matte finish.`
*   **[Character Ref]:** `Aardman style minimalist clay figure, very chubby and stubby proportions with short legs. Bright Musu Yellow (#FFD166) round face with wide-set half-closed tired protruding eyes, a tiny round yellow nose, and a slight frown. Wearing a large oversized thick muted cocoa brown hoodie (#5D4037) with the hood pulled up framing the face, and a front kangaroo pocket.`
*   **[Environment - Inside Screen]:** `Solid Off-White empty background.`
*   **[Environment - Real World]:** `A simple white clay desk with a chunky white clay computer keyboard and a white clay coffee mug.`
*   **[Critical Negatives]:** `CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO SPEECH BUBBLES. The character is completely silent, mouth closed, no speaking, nonverbal, nonlingual, no lip-sync.`

## 🎬 Act 1 - 서론: 코딩을 모르는 나 (0:00 ~ 0:30)

**1. 프롬프트 (Shot 1 - The Endless Loop / txt2vid)**
> **Prompt:** `Medium shot. Stop-motion claymation, Aardman studio style. [Character Ref] The character starts out completely calm, sitting at a simple white clay desk. There is no error block yet. The character picks up a white clay coffee mug, takes a slow sip, places it down, and begins rapidly typing on the white clay computer keyboard. The character dramatically hits the 'Enter' key and waits expectantly for a few seconds. Suddenly, a massive, heavy, bright red clay 'Error' block drops from the sky directly onto the desk with a heavy thud. The character flinches, slumps its shoulders, and looks completely defeated. Soft matte studio lighting. CRITICAL: NO TEXT, NO LETTERS, NO SPEECH BUBBLES.`
