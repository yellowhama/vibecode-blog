# Vibe-Musu Content Templates (2026)

## 1. THE MAGNET (Morning - SEO/Trends)
**Goal:** High-velocity traffic.
**Voice:** Practical, concise, "No-BS."
**Structure:**
1.  **The Hook:** Why this tool/trend matters *today*.
2.  **Quick Setup:** 3-5 steps to implement.
3.  **Code Snippet:** Ready-to-use block with "Copy for AI" button.
4.  **The Verdict:** Is it worth the hype?

## 2. THE BEACON (Lunch - Guru/Technical Depth)
**Goal:** Long-term authority.
**Voice:** Humble Apprentice, technical deconstruction.
**Structure:**
1.  **Guru Context:** Who said it and why they are a Beacon.
2.  **The Mental Model:** Breaking down the core logic.
3.  **X-Ray View:** SVG diagrams or "Scribble" callouts.
4.  **Beacons Section:** Footer links to original sources.

## 3. THE FIELD LOG (Evening - Narrative/MUSU)
**Goal:** Fandom and conversion.
**Voice:** Castaway, gritty implementation scars.
**Structure:**
1.  **The Drift:** A personal story of a failure or discovery.
2.  **The Forging:** How MUSU was used (or built) to solve it.
3.  **The Lesson:** What I learned about the AI ocean.
4.  **The Engine CTA:** Link to musu.pro.
