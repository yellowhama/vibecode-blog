## TL;DR

- Running local AI got me into hardware news. That led me to search for IBM for the first time in my life.
- Found a **DIKW Pyramid** lecture on IBM's YouTube. Data, Information, Knowledge, Wisdom. A framework for where AI actually stands.
- The point: AI is incredible at **Data, Information, and Knowledge**. But **Wisdom**? Not yet.
- The deal: let AI handle the "How." Keep the "Why" and the "What" for yourself.
