# ChatGPT Got Outrun for the First Time: The Real Reason Behind OpenAI's Code Red

## ChatGPT Turns 3. Instead of a Party, an Emergency

December 1, 2025. Exactly three years since ChatGPT launched. Normally you'd light candles on a cake, raise a glass, "We opened the AI era!" That kind of thing.

The mood at OpenAI's office was the opposite.

Monday, December 2nd. Sam Altman sent an internal memo to all employees. **"Code Red."** The highest urgency level inside OpenAI. Plain English: **"Drop everything else. Save ChatGPT."**

Three years ago, another company heard the same words. Google.

When ChatGPT launched in November 2022, Sundar Pichai declared 'Code Red' at Google. They pulled Larry Page and Sergey Brin out of retirement. Reshuffled research teams. Poured every resource into AI product development.

Three years later, the one shouting 'Code Red' is OpenAI.

What happened?
