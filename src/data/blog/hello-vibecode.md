## What is vibecode?

This is where I write about building software with AI. Not theory — practice. Real projects, real tools, real tradeoffs.

I'm building [MUSU](https://musu.pro), an agent runtime for vibe coders. Along the way, I'm learning what works and what doesn't when you let AI drive most of your development workflow.

## What to expect

- **AI-native development** — how to actually ship with Claude, Cursor, and other AI tools
- **Vibe coding experiments** — pushing the boundaries of what AI can build autonomously
- **Technical deep dives** — architecture decisions, performance optimization, infra choices
- **Building in public** — honest updates on what's working and what's not

## Why "vibecode"?

Vibe coding is writing software by vibes — describing what you want, letting AI generate it, iterating fast. It's not about being sloppy. It's about leveraging AI to move at a speed that wasn't possible before.

This blog is the record of that journey.

Let's build.
