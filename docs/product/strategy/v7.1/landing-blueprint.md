## 2. The "Sidekick" Value (Qualitative Feature Descriptions)

### Warden: The Pocket Shotgun

- **Problem**: AI is sometimes confidently wrong. It tries to rm -rf your project root.
- **Solution**: Warden intercepts every command, simulates in sandbox. Destructive = blocked. Suspicious = phone notification.
- **Value**: Stop babysitting your AI. MUSU watches so you don't have to.

### Holodeck: Don't Pay for "Reading"

- **Problem**: Making AI read 100MB log files through the API is money wasted. AI should think, not do grunt work.
- **Solution**: MUSU uses a local virtual shell (grep, awk, etc.) to extract only essential info and pass it to AI.
- **Value**: API costs go to AI intelligence only. Reading and searching grunt work is free locally.

### Intent Lock: Stop the Drift

- **Problem**: You asked to fix one login bug. 20 minutes later AI is refactoring your entire auth system.
- **Solution**: MUSU anchors your original intent. When AI tries to drift, it gets pulled back to the original task.
- **Value**: AI doesn't run wild on your project. The plan finishes the way you started it.

## 3. MUSU Mesh: Your Personal AI Nervous System

The emphasis is on the EXPERIENCE of "my devices become one intelligence", not the connection technology itself.

- **What it is**: Encrypted P2P network connecting your laptop, desktop, and Raspberry Pi into one private cloud.
- **Qualitative Benefit**: Use your powerful home desktop GPU from your phone outside. Data flows ONLY between YOUR devices, not through any central server.
- **Experience**: "All my machines work for me 24/7 as one giant AI team."

## 4. Compliance RAG: Knowledge that Lives with You

- **Context**: Not just "search" but "making AI smarter about YOUR codebase without sending files to the cloud".
- **Reality**: pgvector + ONNX embedding indexes everything on your hard drive.
- **Trust**: Cloud AI can't learn from your code. Only your MUSU OS traces the lineage and provides evidence for answers.
