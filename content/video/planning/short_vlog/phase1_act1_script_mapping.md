# 1분 브이로그/쇼츠용 집중 요약 대본 매핑

**목표:** 원본 블로그 글(`act1-1-en.md`)에서 가장 극적인 문장만 발췌하여 6개의 씬(현재 스토리보드)에 맞게 1분 내외로 영상을 제작합니다.

## 대본 (Narration Script)
*이 대본은 AI 영상 클립의 길이(Timing)를 맞추거나 성우 더빙용으로 활용됩니다.*

---

**[Scene 1. 함정 (The Trap)] - 약 8~10초**
"The first time I opened Claude Code... It didn't just show me the code, it made the files directly. I typed 'npm start,' and a to-do app appeared. Five minutes instead of three hours. That thought... 'I can build anything with this'... was the trap."

**[Scene 2. 파국 (Spaghetti Code)] - 약 10~12초**
"By day three, I had five agents running. A stock screener. Automated slack messages. But then... the whole system stopped. I opened the code. Over ten thousand lines of tangled logic and shell injections. I couldn't read it. I couldn't fix it."

**[Scene 3. 분노 (Fury-Driven Development)] - 약 10~12초**
"Fix one thing, another breaks. Fix that, the first breaks again. Slow. Choppy. 'Good enough' was a lie. I got pissed."

**[Scene 4. 짜증이 스펙이 되다 (The Spec)] - 약 5~7초**
"So what did I do? I asked questions. I fought. I organized. And suddenly... the spec was already there."

**[Scene 5. 스펙의 힘 (Clarity)] - 약 8~10초**
"Once I had the spec, everything changed. Direction meant AI stopped wandering off. Ten thousand lines became three thousand lines of clean code."
