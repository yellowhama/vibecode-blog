# Content Evaluation Framework

Scoring rubric for evaluating blog content readiness for claymation video essay production.

Created: 2026-03-10
Pipeline: MUSU Video Production (nonlingual claymation + VO narration)

---

## Evaluation Axes (5 axes, 5 points each, total 25)

### Axis 1: Hook & Retention Structure

YouTube's algorithm prioritizes viewer satisfaction over raw views. First 3 seconds determine whether viewers stay. Open loops (unanswered questions held until later) are the strongest retention driver.

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **5** | First sentence is a hook. 2+ open loops. Emotional twist/turn-point exists |
| **4** | Strong hook. 1 open loop. Twist exists but predictable |
| **3** | Hook present but weak. Open loop unclear. Mid-section drag risk |
| **2** | Slow opening. No open loop. Linear information flow |
| **1** | Expository intro. No hook. No tension |

**Key signals**: Does the opening make you ask "what happened next?" Can you identify the moment a viewer would click away?

**References**:
- [YouTube Blog: How viewers define quality (2024)](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/how-viewers-define-content-quality-2024/) — emotional connection > production value
- [Fireship analysis](https://read.engineerscodex.com/p/how-fireship-became-youtubes-favorite) — definition in first sentence, code immediately
- [YouTube algorithm 2025](https://metricool.com/youtube-algorithm/) — 50-60% retention = solid, 70% = recommendation tier
- [Storytelling techniques top YouTubers use](https://1of10.com/blog/storytelling-techniques-top-youtubers-use-to-keep-viewers-hooked/) — open loops drive curiosity

### Axis 2: Narrative Arc & Emotional Curve

Story-first approach is the "ultimate algorithm hack" because it taps into what humans fundamentally want. The Five C's framework: Context → Conflict → Climax → Conclusion → Change.

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **5** | All 5 C's present. Clear emotional curve (e.g. excitement → fear → anger → clarity → growth) |
| **4** | 4/5 C's. Emotional curve exists but one transition is weak |
| **3** | Arc exists but climax weak or conclusion rushed |
| **2** | Partial arc. Missing conflict or change |
| **1** | Information list. No arc. No emotional movement |

**Key signals**: Can you draw the emotional curve on paper? Does the ending land differently than the beginning?

**References**:
- [Storytelling frameworks for video](https://maekersuite.com/blog/picking-a-storytelling-framework-for-your-video-script) — Five C's, AIDA, Hero's Journey
- [Kurzgesagt structure analysis](https://medium.com/@prismiqpro/creative-spotlight-how-kurzgesagt-transforms-disorienting-science-youtube-analysis-video-editing-tips-e83f3373d600) — 12 minutes as journey, emotional framing shifts
- [YouTube storytelling trends 2025](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/youtube-storytelling-trends-2025-sean-overton/) — story-first = algorithm hack
- [TubeBuddy: open loops](https://www.tubebuddy.com/blog/youtube-storytelling-trends-2025-sean-overton/) — incomplete thoughts build curiosity

### Axis 3: Visual Metaphor Density

Critical for our pipeline: nonlingual claymation means **every narrative beat must translate to a visible clay action**. No dialogue, no text on screen. The visual IS the story.

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **5** | 70%+ of sentences map to concrete visual actions (object manipulation, character movement, transformation) |
| **4** | 60-70% mappable. Some beats need creative interpretation but workable |
| **3** | 50% mappable. Several abstract/explanatory passages that resist visualization |
| **2** | 30-50% mappable. Heavy rewriting needed for visual adaptation |
| **1** | Mostly abstract/philosophical. Would require invented visual narrative |

**Key signals**: For each sentence, can you describe what the clay character is *doing*? "Tourist with no map" = clay figure looking at blank paper. "Spaghetti code" = tangled clay strings.

**References**:
- [Aardman & claymation storytelling](https://huehd.com/the-best-claymation-animations-of-all-time/) — Wallace & Gromit, Chicken Run — minimal dialogue, maximum visual storytelling
- [Claymation techniques](https://www.ikitmovie.com/claymation-animation/) — "The less dialogue, the easier it travels across the world"
- [Kurzgesagt visual metaphors](https://10.studio/the-incredible-amount-of-work-behind-kurzgesagts-beautiful-animated-videos/) — every script concept gets a visual metaphor planned in storyboard
- [What makes good video essays](https://www.creatorhandbook.net/what-makes-the-best-video-essays-so-great-b9d310ec1b74/) — "blend of style and substance"

### Axis 4: Beat Pacing & Duration Fit

Narration beats must fit video duration constraints. Too dense = video can't keep up. Too sparse = dead air. Target: 2-4 minute videos with 12-24 beats.

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **5** | 2-5 sec per beat. Total 2-4 min. 12-24 natural beats. Breathing room between sections |
| **4** | Mostly good pacing. 1-2 beats too long or too short. Total within 1.5-5 min |
| **3** | Uneven beat lengths. Some 10+ sec passages. Total under 1.5 min or over 5 min |
| **2** | Many long monologue passages. Hard to segment into beats |
| **1** | Wall of text. No natural breakpoints. Can't segment for TTS |

**Key signals**: Read aloud. Does each beat feel like a scene change? Could you cut to a new visual on each beat?

**References**:
- [Video essay art](https://www.storyblocks.com/resources/blog/the-art-of-the-video-essay) — pacing = "shot style, backgrounds, duration creating rhythm"
- [Film festival scoring](https://studylib.net/doc/10784422/film-festival-criteria-developing--1-2-pts--proficient--3...) — pacing develops narrative through time and shot duration
- [YouTube algorithm retention](https://onewrk.com/youtube-algorithm-guide-2025/) — tighter intros, clearer pacing, stronger hooks

### Axis 5: Target Audience Resonance

Our target: **Vibe coders** — people who can't read code but build with AI. They experience the gap between "it runs" and "it works." Content must make them say "that's literally me."

| Score | Criteria |
|---|---|
| **5** | Describes situations target directly experiences. Empathy + actionable takeaway. Shared vocabulary |
| **4** | Relatable to target. Some universal appeal dilutes specificity |
| **3** | Relevant but generic. Could be about any developer/non-developer |
| **2** | Tangentially related. Requires context to connect to target |
| **1** | Irrelevant to target. Too technical or too abstract |

**Key signals**: Would a vibe coder share this video? Would they tag someone saying "watch this"?

**References**:
- [YouTube quality perception](https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/how-viewers-define-content-quality-2024/) — "Quality isn't budgets — it's human connection"
- [Video essay content focus](https://www.creatorhandbook.net/what-makes-the-best-video-essays-so-great-b9d310ec1b74/) — "content focus over narrator persona"
- [Mastering video essays 2025](https://www.descript.com/blog/article/how-to-make-a-video-essay) — thesis-driven, audience-aware structure

## Scoring Guide

| Total | Grade | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **22-25** | A | Production-ready. Start prepro immediately |
| **18-21** | B | Strong candidate. Minor adaptation needed |
| **14-17** | C | Needs rewrite or creative visual reinterpretation |
| **10-13** | D | Major gaps. Consider different content |
| **5-9** | F | Not suitable for this pipeline |

## Reference Channels & Style Benchmarks

| Channel | Style | What to Learn |
|---|---|---|
| **Fireship** | 100-sec explainers, meme cuts | Hook in first sentence. Respect viewer's time. Dense information per second |
| **Kurzgesagt** | 10-min animated essays | Visual metaphor per concept. Music matches pacing. Emotional framing shifts |
| **ThePrimeagen** | Reaction + deep dives | Authentic voice. Energy carries retention. Opinions > neutrality |
| **Aardman (Wallace & Gromit)** | Claymation narrative | Nonlingual storytelling. Character expression through clay. Physical comedy |
| **3Blue1Brown** | Math visualization | Abstract→concrete through animation. "Aha moment" structure |

Our position: **Kurzgesagt's structure + Aardman's aesthetic + Fireship's pacing** for a vibe coder audience.
