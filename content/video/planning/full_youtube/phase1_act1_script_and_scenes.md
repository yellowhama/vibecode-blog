# 풀 타임 유튜브 영상 (블로그 전문 낭독) 대본 및 씬 구분

**목표:** 원본 블로그 글(`act1-1-en.md`) 전체를 나레이션(Voiceover)으로 읽으면서, 이에 어울리는 화이트보드 찰흙 애니메이션 씬을 전면 재구성(Expansion) 합니다.

이 버전은 3~5분에 달하는 긴 호흡이므로, 현재 6개인 씬을 약 **12~15개**의 잘게 쪼개진 시퀀스로 확장해야 합니다.

---

## 🎧 나레이션 대본 및 씬(Scene) 확장 기획안

### [Act 1 - 서론: 코딩을 모르는 나] (0:00 ~ 0:30)
- **Narration:** "Can't code. Not a developer. I've used ChatGPT for over two years. Ask a question, get a code block. Copy it. Paste it in the terminal. Error. Copy the error. Ask again. Get another code block. Paste. Error. Never escaped that loop."
- **New Scene A (The Endless Loop):**
  - 현실(Real World) 책상. 찰흙 캐릭터가 키보드를 치다가 빨간색 'Error' 블록을 맞고 시무룩해지는 무한 루프.

### [Act 2 - 과거의 삽질] (0:30 ~ 0:50)
- **Narration:** "Once I tried making a Python script to clean up Excel files. Took three hours. Didn't understand a single line. It ran. But I had no idea why. Next time I tried it on a different file, it didn't run. Another three hours. That was my 'coding with AI'. Copy-paste, error, copy-paste, error, give up."
- **New Scene B (Excel Nightmare):**
  - 모니터 안(Inside Screen). 초록색 엑셀 모양의 찰흙 덩어리들이 엉켜있고, 캐릭터가 땀을 흘리며 파이썬 뱀(뱀 모양 찰흙)을 끌고 와서 싸우는 슬랩스틱 씬.

### [Act 3 - Claude Code와의 첫 만남] (0:50 ~ 1:20)
- **Narration:** "Heard about vibe coding. Some guy named Karpathy coined it. Didn't know exactly what it was. But people were saying 'you can build apps without knowing code.' Watched some videos. Looked real. Heard about Claude Code. Runs in the terminal. Installed it."
- **New Scene C (Discovery):**
  - 현실 책상. 핸드폰(찰흙)으로 영상을 보던 캐릭터. 눈이 반짝이며 터미널 창을 연다.

### [Act 4 - 함정의 시작 (The Trap)] (1:20 ~ 1:50)
*기존 Storyboard Scene 1 재사용/확장*
- **Narration:** "First time I opened it, I typed 'make me a to-do app' out of habit. With ChatGPT, this is where the code block shows up. But something different happened. Instead of showing me code, it made the files directly. I watched files appear in the terminal. Folders created. Code written. Packages installed. Wait. I don't have to copy-paste? 'Type npm start.' I typed it. A to-do app appeared in my browser. Five minutes. What took three hours with ChatGPT took five minutes."
- **Expanded Scene 1 (The Trap):**
  - 하늘에서 노란색 파일 폴더 찰흙들이 우수수 쏟아져 책상 위에 로봇(투두 앱)을 완성하는 모습.
  - 놀란 캐릭터가 커피를 든 채 행복해함. "That thought... 'I can build anything with this.' That was the trap."

### [Act 5 - 에이전트의 번식] (1:50 ~ 2:30)
*기존 스토리보드에 없던 새로운 시퀀스*
- **Narration:** "Tried one more thing. 'Now a notes app.' Done. 'Connect a database.' Done. 'Login feature.' Done. Everything I asked for worked. Nothing seemed impossible. Wait — maybe impossible things were happening. I just couldn't tell. It ran, so I assumed it worked. Three days in, I had five agents running. Can't really explain what each one does. One collects data, one organizes, one analyzes, one reports, one monitors."
- **New Scene D (Agent Army):**
  - 화면 안(Inside Screen). 1마리였던 노란 로봇이 버튼을 누를 때마다 분열해서 5마리로 불어남.
  - 각기 다른 짐(데이터, 보고서 등)을 들고 돌아다니는 찰흙 로봇들 정신없는 워크플로우.

### [Act 6 - 거미 괴물의 탄생 (Spaghetti Code)] (2:30 ~ 3:00)
*기존 Storyboard Scene 2 재사용/확장*
- **Narration:** "Then something was off. One agent was pulling data slightly wrong. Fine yesterday, numbers are different today. Tweaked the prompt. 'Fetch in this format.' Fixed that. Something else broke. Tweaked again. A feature I had before disappeared. Slack notifications stopped working. Fix one thing, another breaks. Fix that, the first thing breaks again. It wasn't broken. But it wasn't right either. Not fully dead, not fully alive. Stuck in between."
- **Expanded Scene 2 (Spaghetti Code System Failure):**
  - 화면 안. 멀쩡하던 5마리의 로봇들이 서로 엉키며 시커먼 거미 괴물(스파게티 코드)로 융합됨.
  - 에러 창(핏빛 문)이 열리고 징그럽게 발버둥 침.

### [Act 7 - 분노폭발과 깨달음 (Fury-Driven Development)] (3:00 ~ 3:30)
*기존 Storyboard Scene 3 재사용/확장*
- **Narration:** "AI-written code looks convincing. That's the real danger. ... What the hell went wrong? Not the AI's fault. AI did what I told it to. Not the prompt's fault. The problem was deeper. I didn't know what I was building... Developers are locals with built-in GPS. I'm a tourist dropped in a foreign country with no map."
- **Expanded Scene 3 (Real World Slapstick Breakpoint):**
  - 현실 책상. 멘탈이 나간 캐릭터가 주전자처럼 머리 뚜껑이 열려 화이트 찰흙 증기를 뿜어냄.
  - 당황해서 커피를 들이키다 머리와 귀로 커피가 뿜어져 나옴.
  - 좌절하며 책상에 엎드리는데, 텅 빈 칠판(No Map)이 오버랩 됨.

### [Act 8 - 지도 그리기 / 스펙의 탄생 (The Spec)] (3:30 ~ 4:00)
*기존 Storyboard Scene 4 재사용/확장*
- **Narration:** "Developers call that map a 'spec.' But I didn't know how to write one. So what did I do? I got pissed. Asked questions. Fought. Organized. And the spec was already there. Organize your frustration and that's what you get. It doesn't come from a whiteboard in a conference room. It comes from 'why the hell doesn't this work.'"
- **Expanded Scene 4 (Forging the Spec):**
  - 현실(Real World). 공중에 뿜어져 나가던 증기와 커피 파편들이 정지하더니 스스로 조립되어 반듯하게 빛나는 하얀색 찰흙 판(스펙)으로 빚어짐.
  - 씩씩대던 피부색이 노란색으로 돌아오고, 화면 속으로 그 판을 밀어 넣음.

### [Act 9 - 통제와 정리 (Clarity)] (4:00 ~ 4:30)
*기존 Storyboard Scene 5 재사용/확장*
- **Narration:** "Once I had the spec, everything changed. Direction meant AI stopped wandering off. Constraints meant it stopped making the same mistakes. A definition of 'done' meant I could see the finish line. Ten thousand lines became three thousand. I could finally delete things."
- **Expanded Scene 5 (Purification):**
  - 화면 안(Inside Screen). 빛나는 스펙 판을 거미 괴물에게 들이대는 아바타.
  - 빛을 맞은 거미 괴물의 시커먼 찰흙들이 우수수 떨어져 나가고, 아주 날렵하고 정돈된 3개의 작은 노란 로봇으로 재탄생함.

### [Act 10 - 아웃트로 로고 및 결론 (Conclusion)] (4:30 ~ 4:50)
*기존 Storyboard Scene 6 재사용/확장*
- **Narration:** "Better prompts aren't the answer. Better AI tools aren't the answer. Knowing what you're building is the answer. And that answer doesn't come out clean. Specs don't come from conference rooms. They come from the grind."
- **Expanded Scene 6 (Outro Logo):**
  - 화면 안. 작고 깔끔해진 노란 찰흙 로봇이 화면 밖(책상)으로 찰흙 공 4개를 뱉음.
  - 줌 아웃되며 육각형 벌집 모양(Musu Logo)으로 합쳐지고 `MUSU` 글자가 새겨짐.

### 🎥 요약
풀 타임 버전으로 갈 경우:
1. `Scene A, B, C, D` 와 같은 "과거의 엑셀 삽질 / 여러 에이전트의 번식" 등 세팅 모션을 새로 생성해야 합니다.
2. 후반부는 기존 스토리보드의 **슬랩스틱(분노) -> 스펙 생성 -> 괴물 퇴치(정리) -> 로고 아웃트로** 흐름을 그대로 살리되 나레이션 길이에 맞춰 디테일만 조금씩 비디오 프레임으로 연장해주면 됩니다.
