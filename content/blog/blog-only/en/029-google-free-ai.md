# "Google's Free AI Bomb" — You Can Stop Paying for ChatGPT Now

## While Everyone Argued Over $20 Chatbots, Google Gave Away the Whole Store

What's the big debate in AI right now? "Is ChatGPT better or Claude better?" People pay $20 a month for a chatbot and argue about whose tool is superior. But here's the thing.

**Google quietly released 6 expert-level AI tools. All free.**

This isn't email autocomplete. These are tools that do in seconds what used to take researchers a week. While you're still stuck inside one chatbot asking "regenerate that response," the people who know about these are already combining all six to work on a completely different level.

Let's break them down.

## 1. NotebookLM — Your Savior the Night Before an Exam

It's 10 PM. Exam tomorrow. 5 chapters, 30 papers, 100-page PDF study notes staring you in the face. Think you're screwed?

Dump everything into **NotebookLM**. All 5 chapters, your messy lecture notes, a few web links. Upload at once and it reads, indexes, and summarizes instantly.

The real magic starts here. Google calls it "Studio."

Let's be honest. Your eyes hurt. You physically can't read more text. Then don't read. Hit the **Audio Overview** button and NotebookLM turns all your notes into a podcast. Put in earbuds, go for a walk, and "listen" to the chapters.

Audio Overviews have been updated to allow customization and personalization -- you can set custom instructions and adjust the length.

But what if you're a visual learner? Listened to the podcast but nothing stuck? Click **Video Overview**. In 60 seconds you get a short animated explainer. That boring chapter becomes a 7-minute animated summary.

Exam morning. 2 hours left. No time to re-listen. Click **Mind Map**. All 5 chapters shown as one connected map. Key topics, relationships, sub-points on a single page. Final review sheet done.

1 hour before the exam. Standing in the hallway. Need one last check. Click **Quiz**. It instantly generates practice tests based only on your notes. Tests you with your own material. You find out what you don't know before the professor does.

**Free.** What have you done, Google.

## 2. Gemini — The AI That Reads Your Life

OK, so NotebookLM is the exam hack. But what about everyday work? One tool for images, one for writing, one for video, one for email... 10 subscriptions bleeding money.

**Gemini** is the new command center. This isn't just a chatbot. It does something no other AI can.

**It knows you.**

Gmail, Drive, Photos are all connected. That's where the personalization kicks in. Try this prompt:

"Go to my Gmail, find the presentation the designer sent last month, pull 3 key ideas. Then go to my Google Drive, find the whiteboard photo, and summarize that too."

Does it in one shot. Reads your personal email, digs through your entire photo library, reads your messy handwriting in the photos, and gives you a summary. This isn't AI. It's a personal assistant.

There's also **Deep Research**. Not Google Search. You tell Gemini to read, understand, and analyze the entire live internet and bring you a report.

"Give me a 1,000-word deep analysis on the future of India's EV market. Full citations, include the latest 2025 Q3 financial reports."

A complete report comes out. It thought, analyzed, and cited everything on its own.

What about creative work? Say you're building a website for a new coffee brand side project. Don't have the budget for professional photography. Try this prompt:

"Create an ultra-realistic cinematic 16x9 hero image for a new coffee brand website. Top-down shot of black coffee in a minimalist matte black ceramic mug. Mug sits on a dark rustic wood table. A few coffee beans scattered around, one small green plant in soft focus in the background. Lighting is soft morning sunlight coming through a left window."

Veo 3 video generator can produce not just high-quality video footage but realistic sound effects, background noise, and even dialogue.

What comes out isn't a stock photo. It's a custom professional-grade image matching your exact creative brief.

Got the perfect product shot. But it's 2025. You need video for ads, for social feeds. Normally you're back to square one here. Have to hire a videographer.

**Veo** exists. Google's video generation AI. Built right into Gemini. Let's animate that coffee image:

"Animate the last image. Steam rising gently from the coffee mug, camera slowly zooming in over 5 seconds. 1080p. Cozy and professional."

Done. 1080p high-quality video clip. Ready for social media ads.

## 3. Google AI Studio — Turning Your Idea Into an App

Gemini handled creative work. Now the building part. **Google AI Studio** is the new free custom productivity engine.

The name says it all. No coding needed. Describe what you want and how it should feel, and it builds the app.

A prompt like this:

"Build me a simple app. One text input for a product name, a second one for 3 key features. Then a button that generates 5 high-energy Gen-Z style product descriptions for Instagram ads."

Google AI Studio is the fastest place to start building with the Gemini API, leveraging cutting-edge Gemini 2.5 models alongside new generative media models like Imagen and Veo.

A real app comes out. An actual working UI with 2 text boxes and a button. Built an entire tool with just a prompt. Let's test it. Enter "Dark Roast, Smoky, Bold, Intense" and hit generate.

It works. Built a custom tool for an intern in 60 seconds.

There's a pro tip 99% of people don't know. The **Temperature** slider. It's the creativity dial. Slide to 0 and it's a robot. 100% facts. No jokes, no risks. Slide it up and it becomes an artist. A brainstormer. Creative and risk-taking. Adjusting AI's personality before you even start.

**AI Studio Live** is the real jaw-drop moment. The most powerful button: **Screen Share**. A live AI companion.

Say you're a designer. Stuck in Figma. Looking at your design and it's flat.

Open screen share view and click chat. "Studio, look at my screen. This website design is boring. Give me 3 specific things I can fix right now."

**It actually does it.** Looks over your shoulder and tells you how to do your work better.

## 4. Stitch — Making Your Idea Look Good

AI Studio is a beast. Builds custom tools in seconds. But let's be 100% honest. It's ugly. Functional but ugly. Can't put it on a website.

Enter **Stitch**. A tool that creates UI designs from prompts. Let's make a UI for that ad copy generator:

"I need a UI for an ad copy tool. Cozy minimalist vibe for a coffee brand. One text input for product name, one for key features, one generate button. Show 5 results in clean cards below."

Stitch is a new AI tool that generates high-quality UI designs and corresponding front-end code using natural language descriptions or image prompts.

Beautiful output. Fully responsive UI. It understood the cozy vibe. Built the input boxes, button, and 5 cards. Did in 5 seconds what would take a designer all day.

Won't replace a $50K design agency. But for a V1, for a side project -- this is it.

There's **a workflow nobody talks about**. You have the ugly app from AI Studio. You have the pretty UI code from Stitch. Combine them. AI Studio's brain wearing Stitch's face.

**A true zero-coder stack.** Build logic in Studio. Build UI in Stitch. Combine and you've got a world-class product in 10 minutes.

## 5. Pomelli — A Marketing Agency in a Box

OK, now you have a product and a website. The hard part is done. But now you have to sell it. This is where it usually gets expensive. Hire an agency, use freelancers, or burn out guessing what to post.

**Pomelli.** It looks simple. Just one box. But that one box replaces an entire marketing agency.

Pomelli is a new AI marketing tool created by Google Labs and Google DeepMind that helps small businesses create on-brand social media campaigns. Pomelli analyzes your website to learn your brand identity and generates customized campaign ideas and marketing assets.

Paste a URL into that box. Any website. Let's use the coffee shop site we built. Gave it no other information. It scanned the website and built a complete **brand DNA profile**. Colors, fonts, the cozy tone of voice. Understood the brand from one link.

Now put it to work:

"Create a 1-week social media campaign for a fall special latte. 3 Instagram posts, 3 X (formerly Twitter) posts. Cozy, friendly tone."

Pomelli scans your website to create a "Business DNA" profile. It uses this profile to maintain content consistency across channels. It suggests campaign ideas and generates editable marketing assets.

Done. Images use the right brand colors. Captions use the right tone. One click generated a full week of professional, on-brand marketing content.

**A free marketing agency inside a box.**

## 6. Opal — The AI Tool That Makes AI Tools

We've gone from research to creative to building to design to marketing. A 5-tool stack. That alone is an insane free workflow.

But I promised 5 tools and **one secret wildcard**.

**Opal.** AI Studio builds complex apps. Stitch builds websites. Pomelli builds marketing. Opal builds **other AI tools**. A no-code builder for no-code builders.

Opal is a new experimental tool that lets you connect prompts, models, and tools to create and share powerful AI mini-apps -- all with simple natural language and visual editing. Great for rapidly prototyping AI ideas and workflows, demoing concepts as functioning apps, and building custom AI apps that boost work productivity.

Everyone has that one annoying task. For me, it's those ridiculously long, formal, confusing emails from companies I don't know. Instead of wasting time reading them, let's build a tool:

"I need a tool. One big text box for formal emails. Two buttons. Button one: 'Translate to plain English.' Button two: 'Draft a polite rejection reply.'"

The goal was simple: give users the ability to create AI-powered mini-apps using nothing but natural language -- no coding required.

An app was built. It mapped the logic. Understood that 2 buttons mean 2 separate tasks. This is just a blueprint. Hit **Publish** and it goes live. A real app. Bookmark it and use it every day.

Paste in a terrible email. Click "translate to plain English." Click "draft polite rejection." Done.

Think about what just happened. You didn't use AI. **You built an AI tool custom-fit to your own life.** Just by describing it. Tweet idea generator, resume helper, meal planner. Anything.

**That's the unfair advantage I promised.**
